Our Approach

We invest first
in people.

We place little weight on hype, signaling, or who else is involved, and focus on whether something can be built, scaled, and sustained over time.

Stage is secondary to the quality of the people, the clarity of execution, and whether a business can scale and endure in the real world. Our investments span AI, software, fintech, real estate, and consumer technology.

01

Analytical & Disciplined

We underwrite carefully, think in portfolios, and take a long-term view. We are comfortable making concentrated bets when there is conviction in the team and the path forward.

02

Founder Friendly

We are generally hands-off unless asked. When helpful, we are glad to share perspective, make introductions, and collaborate with other investors.

03

Multi-Stage

We invest across stages, from seed through late-stage, taking direct positions and gaining exposure to leading private companies through funds and SPVs.

About

Led by Abe Varughese.

Abe Varughese
Founder & Managing Partner

Alpha Venture Capital is led by Abe Varughese, a graduate of NYU Stern, who oversees the firm's investment strategy and portfolio decisions.

His background spans business operations, healthcare, and investing. He is President and Co-Founder of several companies and serves on the boards of private and nonprofit organizations.

Abe has led high-level initiatives at Northwell Health, the largest private employer in New York, working closely with senior executives across inpatient and outpatient settings.

He also leads private investment companies active in public equities and real estate investment and development, with a patient, long-term philosophy across every asset class.
